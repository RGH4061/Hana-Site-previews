การประกอบด้วยเทคโนโลยีการวางชิ้นส่วนบนผิวแผงวงจร (SMT)

Hana ให้บริการการประกอบแบบ SMT ระยะละเอียด (fine-pitch) ทั้งสองด้านของแผงวงจร สำหรับผลิตภัณฑ์ที่ใช้ชิ้นส่วนตั้งแต่ชิ้นส่วนพาสซีฟขนาด 01005 ไปจนถึง micro-BGA และ WLCSP ที่มีระยะห่างระหว่างจุดต่อ (pitch) 0.2 mm โดยมีการตรวจสอบครีมบัดกรี (SPI) การตรวจสอบความสมบูรณ์ของตะกั่วบัดกรีด้วยระบบตรวจสอบอัตโนมัติ (3D AOI) และการตรวจสอบ (X-ray) เป็นส่วนหนึ่งของกระบวนการมาตรฐาน

Capability overview

SMT process

A complete SMT process from solder-paste printing through placement, reflow, inspection and de-paneling, with additional soldering options where the product requires them.

  • ›Screen printing
    Solder paste / epoxy, accuracy ±25 µm
  • ›Solder paste inspection
    3D inspection of volume, shape and X-Y position, 10 µm high-resolution camera
  • ›Component mounting
    Up to 15 mm height, accuracy ±18 µm
  • ›SMT inspection
    3D automated optical inspection, 12 µm high-resolution camera
  • ›Reflow soldering
    Hot air / N₂ with oxygen analyzer for PPM oxygen control
  • ›Vacuum reflow oven Key capability
    For low solder-void requirements
  • ›DI cleaning
    Supports water-soluble solder paste
  • ›2D and 3D X-ray inspection Key capability
    3D X-ray for BGA, BTC and PoP
Fine-pitch capability

Fine-pitch assembly is supported within the standard SMT process, covering ultra-small passives, array packages and large-format QFP.

  • ›Smallest chip size
    0201 mm or 01005 inch
  • ›BGA ball pitch
    0.2 mm
  • ›BGA ball size
    0.1 mm – 0.76 mm
  • ›QFP, connector fine pitch
    0.4 mm
Traceability

Every unit is traceable individually, with material, machine, tooling and process parameter data recorded through the SMT line.

  • ›Material
    Component, PCB, solder paste, epoxy, glue, etc.
  • ›Machine
    Screen printer, SPI, PnP, reflow oven, AOI, cleaning, X-ray
  • ›Tooling
    Stencil, squeegee, fixture
  • ›Process parameter
    Screen printing, SPI, PnP, reflow soldering, AOI, cleaning, X-ray

Why run SMT with Hana

Qualified SMT capability across multiple countries

SMT is qualified in China, Thailand, Cambodia and the USA, so a program can be dual-sourced from multiple countries within the same company to support capacity, regional production requirements and supply-chain resilience. Each additional site is qualified against the customer's requirements through documented first-article qualification, using the same process capability.

  • ›Dual-source from multiple countries within the same company
  • ›Second-site qualification through documented first-article approval
  • ›Capacity and regional production options
  • ›Common process capability from NPI through volume production

SMT within a broader EMS and OSAT capability

Hana combines SMT with wafer processing, die attach, wire bond, chip-on-board, flip chip and full box-build assembly within the same group. This allows engineering teams to bring more of the manufacturing flow together where it makes sense, supported by DFM review before production introduction.

  • ›EMS and OSAT within one group
  • ›DFM and early-stage engineering collaboration
  • ›Integration from SMT through box build and drop-ship
  • ›Customer IP protected under ISO 27001

Frequently asked questions

What is the smallest component Hana can place on an SMT line?+
Hana places 01005 passives and supports micro-BGA and WLCSP attachment to 0.2 mm pitch, with QFP packages to 0.4 mm pitch.
Does Hana inspect every board, or only high-reliability work?+
Solder-paste inspection, 3D AOI and X-ray are part of the standard SMT process, with full material, unit and product traceability. These controls are not limited to automotive or medical programs.
Can an SMT program be qualified at more than one Hana site?+
Yes. SMT is qualified in China, Thailand, Cambodia and the USA, so a program can be dual-sourced from multiple countries within the same company. Each site is qualified separately against the customer's requirements through documented first-article qualification.
What board and panel sizes can Hana handle?+
PCB panels up to 440 × 600 mm are supported, with convection reflow in air or nitrogen and vacuum nitrogen reflow available.
What quality and certification systems cover SMT?+
Customer IP is protected under ISO 27001. Hana also operates dedicated medical and automotive assembly lines within fully ESD-controlled facilities.
