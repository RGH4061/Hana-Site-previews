Frequently asked questions

How is a tire tag built to take being embedded in a tire?

A tire tag is embedded in rubber during vulcanization, so it must withstand cure temperatures above 150°C and then repeated flex and temperature exposure throughout the tire's service life. The antenna, chip attach and encapsulation materials are selected for this environment, and read performance can be verified after cure.

How does tire-tag manufacturing relate to Hana's wider RFID capability?

Tire tags use the same core inlay, strap-attach and roll-to-roll manufacturing capability as Hana's wider RFID portfolio, with additional material and process requirements for embedding and cure.

Where does Hana build tire tags?

Tire tags are built in Lamphun, Thailand and Ohio, USA, with the site confirmed per program.

What frequency and protocol do the tags use?

UHF across 860–960 MHz to ISO 18000-63 / EPC Gen 2. Other protocols can be considered to program requirements.

Can Hana support the antenna design as well as production?

Yes. Hana can work with the customer on antenna geometry and encapsulation material selection so the tag can be qualified against the required tire compound, geometry and production process before volume manufacturing.

What quality systems cover tire tags?

ISO 9001 applies group-wide and IATF 16949 applies to automotive work. Every tag is encoded and read-verified, with per-unit traceability.
