Frequently asked questions

What is the smallest package you build?+
0.6 × 1.0 mm, available both as a leaded SOD723 and as a leadless DFN.
How thin can the package be?+
Leadless packages start at 0.37 mm nominal. The thinnest in production is a 0.30 mm optical LGA, which is a clear-molded part rather than a standard body.
Can ultra-small parts be automotive-qualified?+
Yes. SC70, SOT553, uDFN, uTDFN, sTDFN and TDFN are automotive-qualified, and SOT23 3L runs at Grade 0.
What is the smallest die you can handle?+
10 × 10 mils, placed to ±10 µm, with bond pad pitch down to 45 µm. Below that the constraint is usually the pad pitch rather than the die.
What if our design doesn't match a standard footprint?+
Hana can develop a custom package, including the leadframe, package outline, and footprint, with thermal and warpage simulation before tooling is cut. If a standard footprint already fits, we build to it.
