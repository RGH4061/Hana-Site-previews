Frequently asked questions

Do you offer wettable flanks?+
Yes, in two forms: a step cut for full-lead QFN and a single cut for dimple-on-lead QFN. Both run in production, with chemical de-burr to 50 µm.
What sizes and lead counts do you run?+
DFN from 0.6 × 1.0 mm, QFN to 12 × 12 mm, and LGA from 1.0 × 1.5 × 0.5 mm. The full standard range is searchable in the package reference on this page.
Can you put more than one die in a leadless package?+
Yes. Two FETs and an IC side by side in a single body, passing MSL1 with zero top delamination on CSAM.
What is the difference between the two wettable flank cuts?+
The step cut suits a full-lead QFN, where material is removed to form a step that supports solder wetting along the lead edge. The single cut suits a dimple-on-lead design and uses de-burr chemistry to leave the lead clean.
Does a thinner body change what you can put inside it?+
It changes the die thickness and the loop clearance, not the package family. The thickness ladder runs from 0.37 mm to 0.90 mm as standard, with film-assist molding used to control planarity and warpage in thin package bodies.
