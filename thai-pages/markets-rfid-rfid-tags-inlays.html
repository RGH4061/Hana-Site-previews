Frequently asked questions

What RFID frequencies does Hana manufacture for?

Hana's RFID manufacturing capability covers LF, HF and UHF. Inlay and tag production centers on UHF and HF, with the applicable band and format confirmed per program.

What does roll-to-roll RFID production mean?

RFID inlays and tags are produced on a continuous web, with antenna, chip or strap attach, lamination, converting and test integrated into the production line. The finished material is then supplied on a roll or converted into the required tag format.

Is every tag tested before it ships?

Yes. Every inlay and tag is encoded and read-verified before shipment, with unique-ID handling and per-unit traceability.

What makes an industrial RFID tag different from a label?

Industrial RFID tags use a built construction designed for the mounting surface and operating environment. This can include encapsulation, ruggedized materials and on-metal antenna designs that allow a UHF tag to remain readable when mounted against metal.

What read range can industrial UHF tags achieve?

Read range depends on tag construction, mounting surface and reader configuration, so it is established for each program. Every tag is encoded and read-verified before shipment.
