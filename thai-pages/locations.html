Structural advantages for your supply chain

Geographic diversification
Manufacturing across four countries gives genuine supply-chain resilience against geo-political headwinds — including a domestic US option and a diversified production strategy.
Manufacturing flexibility across sites
Dual-source across one company without re-qualifying a new supplier. Our sites are diversified in capability mix and wage structure to strike the right balance for our customers.
The strictest quality, at every site
One quality system across the group, with certifications matched to the work — IATF 16949 for automotive, ISO 13485 for medical, and a TAA-compliant US site for defense and government supply chains.
