Frequently asked questions

Common questions from optical and sensor engineering and procurement teams.

What does Hana build for optical and sensor products?

Hana manufactures camera modules, including infrared modules, MEMS and optical sensors such as proximity, ambient light, color and time-of-flight devices. Depending on the program, these can be assembled and tested as complete optical or sensor units.

Why does optical and sensor assembly need cleanroom and alignment?

Optical and sensor products can require micron-level alignment and controlled protection from contamination and static discharge. Hana uses cleanroom manufacturing, controlled ESD handling and active optical alignment for camera modules where required by the product.

Which sites serve optical and sensor programs?

Camera modules are manufactured in Ohio, USA, while proximity, ambient light and MEMS sensor assembly runs in Ayutthaya, Thailand. Supporting board-level assembly is also available in Lamphun.

Can Hana handle miniature, sensitive sensor assemblies?

Yes. Hana supports miniature optical and MEMS sensor assemblies using low-stress die attach, controlled handling and cleanroom manufacturing processes.
