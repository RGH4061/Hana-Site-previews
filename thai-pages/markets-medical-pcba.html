What we specialize in

The processes below run on dedicated medical lines under an ISO 13485 quality system.

01Flex and rigid-flex circuits
Two- and four-layer adhesiveless flex down to approximately 5 mil total thickness, with 2 mil lines and spaces and 2 mil blind vias.
02Flip chip and miniature passives
Fine-pitch bare die in solder-bump, gold-bump and gold-plated formats, placed on flex or rigid board alongside 0201 and 01005 passives.
03Underfill, coating, folding and depaneling
Automated underfill and coating dispense with thermal or air cure, controlled multi-step folding into the device shape, and laser depaneling and marking.
04Documented, traceable build
Controlled work instructions, formal change management and material, process and parameter traceability recorded throughout production.

See SMT Assembly for the placement accuracy, solder paste inspection, optical inspection, X-ray and test processes applied across these assemblies.

Turnkey service

Starting from IC through to packed medical device

Hana can run the complete build of a miniature medical device within one manufacturing program. Flex assembly, flip-chip attach, miniature passive placement, underfill and coating, folding and laser depaneling are followed by box build, functional test and final packing.

  • Box buildIntegration of the assembly into the finished device, including enclosure and housing assembly, cabling and wire harnesses, potting and sealing.
  • Functional testFinished devices are tested on product-specific rigs developed in-house.
  • Packing and shipmentFinished units can be packed to the customer’s specification and shipped directly or drop shipped.

Where individual processes are performed at another Hana site, they remain within the group's controlled manufacturing and change-management structure.

Frequently asked questions

What medical products can Hana build boards for?

Hana manufactures boards for patient-monitoring and diagnostic equipment, together with miniature flex and flip-chip circuits for hearing, wearable and drug-delivery devices. Assemblies can be supplied as PCBAs or integrated into the finished device depending on program scope.

Can Hana assemble flip chip and flex for medical devices?

Yes. Fine-pitch flip-chip die using solder-bump, gold-bump or gold-plated formats can be assembled on multi-layer adhesiveless flex alongside 0201 and 01005 passives, with underfill, coating and multi-step folding available within the same manufacturing program. Flip chip bonding methods are described on Precision Flip Chip Bonding.

What does ISO 13485 mean for how a board is built?

Under ISO 13485, each build follows controlled work instructions, and changes to materials or process go through formal change management. Inspection results are recorded, and materials, process steps and parameters remain traceable through the build.
