Ruggedized manufacturing for long product lifecycles

Industrial and IoT products operate in cabinets, production environments and outdoor enclosures, and often remain in production for many years. Both conditions shape how the product is assembled and how changes are controlled once it is in production.

Ruggedized build

Conformal coating, mixed-technology assembly and through-hole processes for products exposed to heat, vibration, moisture and mechanical loading.

Long-program support

Controlled change management and obsolescence support for industrial programs that remain in production over extended product lifecycles.

Multi-site manufacturing

Qualified processes across Hana sites in Thailand and China provide additional manufacturing capacity and supply-continuity options within the group.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions from industrial and IoT engineering and procurement teams.

What does Hana build for industrial and IoT?

Hana manufactures ruggedized PCBAs and complete box-build products for industrial and IoT equipment, including smart metering devices such as water meters, heat meters and heat cost allocators, energy and environmental monitors, controllers and connected IoT sensors. It also builds industrial power modules and RFID asset-tracking tags. Depending on the program, the scope can extend from board assembly through to complete, tested product assembly.

Does Hana build smart meters?

Yes. Hana can assemble smart water meters, heat and energy meters and heat cost allocators, together with the radio communication modules and gateways used to read them remotely. The scope runs from the PCBA through to the sealed, tested meter, with functional and wireless test set to the program’s requirements. Connected meters and monitors are covered on Industrial IoT devices.

How does Hana support long-running industrial programs?

Hana supports long-running industrial programs through controlled change management, component obsolescence support and documented manufacturing processes. Ruggedization options including conformal and parylene coating are available where required by the product environment.

Which sites serve industrial programs?

Lamphun and Ayutthaya in Thailand and Jiaxing in China. An industrial program can be qualified at more than one of these plants to dual-source within the same company.

What ruggedized finishing can Hana apply?

Conformal and parylene coating and harsh-environment finishing are available within our industrial box-build scope. The finish set is selected per program and confirmed per site during the program review.
