Electronics manufacturing in Zhejiang Province

Jiaxing is Hana’s China manufacturing hub, serving customers in access control, locking technology and broader industrial electronics. The site runs OSAT services and IC packaging alongside full PCBA, box build, DFM and NPI, within one vertically integrated operation. OSAT work here includes power discretes and IGBT and SiC power modules; see Power Packages for package sizes and qualification.

Several customer relationships here have run more than two decades. That is the standard we measure ourselves by. We work alongside your engineering team from first article through production ramp.

Frequently asked questions

What does Hana's Jiaxing plant manufacture?+
Jiaxing is Hana's manufacturing plant in China. It runs IC packaging and test, including power packages, alongside SMT, chip on board, box build and RFID inlay production on automated lines. Products include access control readers, electronic lock assemblies, industrial IoT gateways, GPS modules and IGBT and SiC power modules.
Which certifications does the Jiaxing plant hold?+
ISO 9001, IATF 16949, ISO 13485, ISO 14001, IECQ QC 080000 and ISO 45001. Full certificates are available on request.
Where is the Jiaxing plant?+
In the Jiaxing Economic and Technological Development Zone, Zhejiang Province, in the Yangtze River Delta about 100 km southwest of Shanghai. The nearest port is Shanghai, and the nearest airports are Hangzhou Xiaoshan (about 80 km) and Shanghai Pudong (about 125 km).
