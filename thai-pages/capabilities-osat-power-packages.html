OSAT · แพ็กเกจสำหรับอุปกรณ์กำลัง (IGBT, SiC และ GaN)

แพ็กเกจสำหรับอุปกรณ์กำลังและโมดูลกำลังไฟฟ้า (Power packages and modules)

Hana ประกอบเซมิคอนดักเตอร์กำลังชนิดดิสครีต (power discrete) และโมดูลกำลังไฟฟ้า (power module) ชนิด IGBT, SiC และ GaN ตั้งแต่ดิสครีตแบบ TO-247, D2PAK-7L และ TOLL ไปจนถึงโมดูลขนาด 62 mm สำหรับงานออกแบบที่ประสิทธิภาพการระบายความร้อน การเป็นฉนวนทางไฟฟ้า และการรองรับกระแสเป็นตัวกำหนดโครงสร้างของแพ็กเกจ

Capability overview

Power discretes

Production-ready high-power discrete formats.

  • ›TO-247 in 2L, 3L and 4L, plus 4L notched
  • ›D2PAK-7L, TOLL and PQFN 8×8
  • ›AEC-Q101 qualified across the TO-247 family and D2PAK-7L
  • ›SiC diodes and MOSFETs, SJ MOS, and FRFET
Power modules

Established module footprints for high-power applications.

  • ›34 mm and 62 mm
  • ›EconoDual 3, Econo and PIM
  • ›Easy 1B, 2B and 3B
  • ›HP1 and HPD for traction inverters
  • ›Custom SiP assembly with SiC on DBC
Thermal & interconnect

Thermal and interconnect choices that protect the device rating.

  • ›Copper-clip interconnect in place of source wire bonds
  • ›Dual-cool construction on PDFN 8×8, PLP 8×8, and TO-247TE
  • ›Ceramic DBC substrates for electrical isolation
  • ›Vacuum reflow to control thermal-path voiding
GaN and wide-bandgap

Packaging for GaN devices in chargers and power conversion.

  • ›GaN in power QFN 5×6, with a copper-clip source connection
  • ›GaN IPMs for battery chargers and SiP power converters
  • ›GaN and SiC on DBC for EV chargers
  • ›Laser grooving and dicing for SiC and GaN wafers
Top-side cooling

Packages with a thermal pad on the top surface, so heat is taken into a heatsink rather than through the board.

  • ›TSPAK (TO263-7L V2), a Hana patented package design, AEC-Q101 qualified
  • ›Leadframe or DBC substrate; the DBC version gives higher thermal performance
  • ›TOLT, QDPAK, PQPAK and TSMPAK outlines

Frequently asked questions

Which module formats do you assemble?+
34 mm, 62 mm, EconoDual 3, Econo, PIM, Easy 1B, 2B and 3B, and HP1 and HPD for traction inverters, plus SiC on DBC built into a custom SiP where a standard format does not fit.
Which power discretes are AEC-Q101 qualified?+
The TO-247 family (2L, 3L, 4L and 4L notched), D2PAK-7L and TSPAK are AEC-Q101 qualified. TOLL and PQFN 8×8 are qualified to industrial requirements. Maximum die size for each package is listed in the power package reference above and can be adjusted after design and process evaluation.
How do you manage thermal performance in a wide-bandgap package?+
Copper clip interconnect instead of wire bonds, dual-cooled pads exposed top and bottom, and ceramic DBC substrates. Voids in the thermal path are held under 5% by vacuum reflow.
What voltage and isolation class can you assemble to?+
Devices to the 1200 V class, with built-in isolation above 2500 V on the TO-247TE outline.
Do you package GaN devices?+
Yes. GaN devices can be packaged in power QFN 5×6 and in custom IPMs for battery chargers, SiP power converters and EV chargers, including GaN and SiC on DBC. Laser grooving and dicing are qualified for SiC and GaN wafers.
What reliability testing can you run on a power module?+
Power cycling, thermal cycling and reliability sequences are available to the programme qualification plan. The test set is agreed per programme and confirmed per site.
What is this used for?+
EV and hybrid drivetrains, rail traction, solar and wind inverters, industrial drives, and data center power.
Can you build a module footprint that isn't one of the standards?+
Yes. SiC on DBC assembled into a custom SiP. If the outline itself has to be drawn, that runs through package design and simulation before any tooling is cut.
