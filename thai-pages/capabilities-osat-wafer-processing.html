Capability overview

Wafer thinning

Mechanical backgrind with post-grind stress relief and handling methods suited to thin and fragile wafers.

  • ›Thin-wafer handling on carriers
  • ›Device- and thickness-specific process recipes
Singulation

Laser grooving, blade dicing and full laser cutting are selected according to wafer material and device requirements.

  • ›Laser grooving with blade dicing — qualified for low-K and GaN
  • ›Full laser cut on SiC, sapphire and glass
  • ›8 µm laser spot, eight-beam split
  • ›Saw street down to 10 µm
  • ›Dice-before-grind option
Preparation and inspection

Mounting, release and post-dice inspection are completed with wafer-map data retained for downstream assembly.

  • ›Wafer-map handling into assembly
  • ›Full material and lot traceability

Frequently asked questions

What does Hana’s wafer processing cover?+
Wafer processing covers backgrind, singulation by laser grooving with blade dicing or full laser cut, and wafer-level inspection before die attach and package assembly.
Which wafer materials can you dice?+
Laser grooving with blade dicing is qualified for low-K and GaN. SiC, sapphire and glass use full laser cutting, with an 8 µm laser spot and eight-beam split, and saw streets down to 10 µm.
Can you handle thin and fragile wafers?+
Yes. Thinning includes post-grind stress relief and carrier-based handling, with dicing recipes adjusted to reduce chipping on brittle or thinned die.
Do you keep traceability from wafer to package?+
Yes. Wafer-map and lot data are carried through singulation into assembly, maintaining material and lot traceability.
How is wafer processing controlled?+
Processing takes place in cleanroom, ESD-controlled areas, with device-specific process recipes qualified on first articles before volume production.
