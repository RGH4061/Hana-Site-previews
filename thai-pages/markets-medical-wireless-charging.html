Frequently asked questions

Why does wireless charging matter for medical devices?

Wireless charging provides a charging interface without requiring a physical connector on the device enclosure. For hearing aids and wearable medical devices, Hana can manufacture the associated charging case or module, including coil assembly, PCBA, enclosure integration and functional test. For the broader wireless-charging picture, see Consumer Electronics.

Can Hana integrate charging into a sealed device?

Yes. Coil winding and assembly, board assembly and box build are available within Hana, allowing the charging assembly and associated device to be manufactured and tested within the group.

What quality system covers medical charging assemblies?

Medical charging assemblies run on dedicated medical lines under an ISO 13485 quality system, with documented process control, change management and traceability applied throughout production.
