How Hana shares are listed and traded, and how the company approaches dividends.

What is Hana's dividend policy?+

The Company has a policy to pay dividends to shareholders at a rate of not less than 30% of its consolidated net profit, after deduction of corporate income tax and allocation to statutory reserves as required by law. The actual dividend payment shall depend on the Company’s operating results, financial position, cash flow, investment plans, and other factors as deemed appropriate by the Board of Directors, and shall be in compliance with applicable laws and regulations. Accordingly, the dividend payout ratio may be lower or higher than the above rate, and may exceed 100% of consolidated net profit where deemed appropriate by the Board of Directors.

The dividend policy of the subsidiaries is to pay dividends to the Company based on their operating results, financial position, cash flow, investment plans, and business requirements, as deemed appropriate by their respective boards of directors and in compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

When does Hana pay dividends?+

Dividends are typically considered alongside the full-year results and approved at the Annual General Meeting, with payment following the record date set in the SET announcement. Any interim dividend is announced separately.

Does Hana offer a dividend reinvestment plan?+

Hana does not currently operate a dividend reinvestment plan. Dividends are paid in cash to registered shareholders.

Financial Reporting

When and how Hana reports its results, and where to find the underlying documents.

When does Hana's financial year end?+

Hana's financial year runs from 1 January to 31 December. The company reports results on a quarterly basis in line with SET requirements.

How often does Hana report results?+

Hana publishes quarterly financial statements together with a Management’s Discussion & Analysis (MD&A), and a full-year Annual Report (Form 56-1 One Report). Each release is announced to the SET and made available on the company's Investor Relations pages.

Where can I find Hana's annual report and financial statements?+

The latest Annual Report (56-1 One Report) is available on the Annual Reports page. Quarterly statements and MD&A are on the Investor News page.

How can I follow Hana's SET announcements?+

All official disclosures are filed with the Stock Exchange of Thailand and republished on our Investor News page as downloadable documents, grouped by year.

Company & Listing

Background on the company behind the listing and how to get in touch.

What does Hana Microelectronics do?+

Hana Microelectronics is an electronics manufacturing services (EMS) and semiconductor assembly & test (OSAT) provider. The group manufactures printed circuit board assemblies, integrated circuit packaging, RFID and smart-tag products, and sensor and optical modules for customers across automotive, industrial, medical, telecommunications, and other markets.

Where does Hana operate?+

Hana is headquartered in Thailand and operates manufacturing facilities across Thailand, China, Cambodia, and the United States. Details of each site are on the Locations pages.

When was Hana listed on the SET?+

Hana Microelectronics has been listed on the Stock Exchange of Thailand since 01 Feb 1993. Listing details are summarized in the Annual Report.

How do I contact the Investor Relations team?+

Investor inquiries are handled by the Corporate Affairs team. Contact details and the registered office address are on the Investor Events & Contact page.
