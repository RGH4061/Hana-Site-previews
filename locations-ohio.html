Hana RFID and EMS manufacturing in the USA

Operating as Hana Technologies Inc, we are leaders in the identification/IoT industry, offering an array of RFID products to the unique demands of our customers as well as lean EMS and contract manufacturing services.

We combine materials knowledge and RFID design capability with global manufacturing capacity, so customers can be supported locally. Hana RFID partners with converters and system integrators, who receive exclusive access to our products. Our RFID solutions are used for tagging and tracking, and can also support connectivity, service efficiency and customer service.

A domestic-US manufacturing option, with IATF for automotive, ITAR certified for defense-related work and ARC-certified for RFID inlay design and manufacturing.

Frequently asked questions

What does Hana's Ohio plant manufacture?+
The Ohio plant in Solon operates as Hana Technologies, Inc. It designs and manufactures UHF, HF and LF RFID inlays, straps and tire tags, and provides EMS including SMT, chip on board and box build, together with camera modules and precision optical assemblies.
Which certifications does the Ohio plant hold?+
ISO 9001:2015 and IATF 16949, together with ITAR certification for defense-related work. RFID inlay design and manufacturing at the plant is certified under the ARC (Auburn) program.
Where is the Ohio plant?+
At 29000 Aurora Road, Solon, Ohio 44139, USA, in greater Cleveland, about 30 km from Cleveland Hopkins International Airport.

Contact Hana Technologies in Ohio

For RFID inlays, tire tags and EMS programs in the USA, contact the Hana Technologies team in Solon, Ohio directly through the Hana RFID website.
