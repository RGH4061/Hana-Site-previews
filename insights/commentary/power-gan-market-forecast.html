Power GaN market forecast: what the 2031 outlook means for packaging

Key takeaways

A market research report published in August 2026 projects the power GaN market reaching $3.5bn by 2031, with automotive and data center power growing fastest. Segment timing, a wider foundry base and package requirements are the open questions.

A market research report published in August 2026 describes the power GaN market moving beyond consumer chargers into data center, automotive and industrial power. Power GaN refers to gallium nitride transistors used in power conversion, where they switch faster than silicon devices and can support higher power density. For a power program, the forecast indicates which GaN power devices may reach volume first.

What does the power GaN market forecast show?

The report projects the power GaN market reaching $3.5bn in 2031, a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 35%.

  • Consumer and mobile electronics remain the largest segment by revenue, expected to reach $1.7bn in 2031.
  • Automotive grows fastest at a 57% CAGR, supported by onboard chargers and 48V architectures.
  • Telecom and infrastructure, driven by AI data center demand, grows at a 45% CAGR to $750m.
  • The report describes humanoid robots as a possible growth source for GaN devices after 2030, because their power density needs suit GaN, although volumes today are negligible.

A second published forecast reaches $3.17bn by 2032 at a 26.3% CAGR, so totals depend on scope and base year.

Where the practical constraints sit

Demand growth becomes shipped product if wafer supply, device qualification and packaging keep pace. The report describes a wider foundry base for GaN, with production once concentrated at one foundry spreading to additional foundries, including one qualifying US production. Integrated device manufacturers keep adding their own capacity, often through acquisitions. The report cites 650V devices on 200mm GaN-on-silicon wafers as one platform under development with a foundry partner. Each new source needs separate qualification, so device availability may vary by supplier while that work continues. Fast-switching GaN devices place demands on the package, since loop inductance and the thermal path from die to board affect how much of the device's performance a design can use.

What we're watching

These open questions will show how the forecast develops over the next few years and which constraints ease first:

  • Does automotive, which has the highest growth rate, reach volume on the report's timeline, or does data center power scale first?
  • How quickly does the wider foundry base reach consistent yield, and how long does customer qualification take for each new supplier?
  • What do 48V and onboard charger designs require from GaN packaging in terms of thermal performance and switching-loop inductance?
  • How do customers split volume across suppliers while customers qualify the newer GaN sources?
  • Do humanoid robots create volume after 2030, and would their power density requirements call for new package formats?

Why it matters for manufacturing buyers

The power GaN market forecast suggests that data center and automotive programs will compete for foundry output and package capacity. Hana's OSAT services include dicing of GaN wafers and GaN power packages. Where a program dual-sources from multiple countries within the same company, each site still needs separate qualification against the customer's requirements. Starting package selection early gives qualification more time to complete.
