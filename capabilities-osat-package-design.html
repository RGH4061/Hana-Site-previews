Package Design & Simulation Services

Hana’s packaging design and simulation services help customers take sensor package concepts through to a manufacturable, volume-ready design. Using our packaging expertise, manufacturing capabilities and process IP, we can shrink, reconfigure and optimise existing designs to improve package size, assembly efficiency, performance and manufacturability at scale.

Case study · Package design

Halving the footprint of an optical sensor module

A customer brought us a working optical LiFi sensor module built around a machined metal housing and a flex-cable PCB. It performed, but it was large, slow to assemble, and expensive to build at volume.

Through Hana's advantaged packaging team, we re-engineered the package from the optics outward resulting in a substantial improvement.

02

Hana's engineering development

03

New product design

A compact integrated LiFi light antenna module.

Design

  • VCSEL, ASIC and photo detector integrated inside
  • Integrated optical window and shield
  • One-piece metal shield, EMI and ESD
  • Direct board mounting, no flex cable

The same route is open to any optical or sensor package: benchmark what you have today, simulate the optics, then design the package around volume production.
