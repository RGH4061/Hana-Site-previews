Controlled manufacturing for regulated medical devices

Medical manufacturing requires documented process control, traceability and formal change management throughout production. Hana operates dedicated medical lines under an ISO 13485 quality system for hearing aids, sensors, drug-delivery electronics and diagnostic assemblies. Qualified medical manufacturing processes are available across multiple Hana sites, providing additional capacity and supply-continuity options within the group.
