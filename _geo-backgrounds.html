Geometric backgrounds

Large flat shapes in the Hana blue palette, drifting slowly behind a dark band. The shapes blend lighter where they overlap. Use the motion version on the site through css/hana-geo-motion.css and js/hana-geo-motion.js, or a still image from images/backgrounds/geo/. Motion stops for visitors who have reduced motion set. Reference page only; not linked from the site.

Motion: band

For a CTA or section band, roughly 280–500px tall. Midnight is the version used on the homepage preview.

data-hana-geo="band" data-geo-theme="midnight"

Midnight band

data-geo-theme="blue"

Hana Blue Deep band

data-geo-theme="navy"

Navy band

Motion: strip

For thin dividers and bars up to about 150px tall. The shapes are centred on the strip so their edges cross it. Used on the homepage preview for the top utility bar (36px), the Quality & certifications band (about 144px) and the footer divider (40px).

data-hana-geo="strip" data-geo-theme="midnight" style="height:40px"
height:5px
data-geo-theme="blue" style="height:40px"

Markup

<link rel="stylesheet" href="css/hana-geo-motion.css"/>
<script src="js/hana-geo-motion.js"></script>

<!-- Section or CTA band -->
<section data-hana-geo="band" data-geo-theme="midnight"> … content … </section>

<!-- Divider strip -->
<div data-hana-geo="strip" data-geo-theme="midnight" aria-hidden="true" style="height:40px"></div>
AttributeValuesNotes
data-hana-geoband | stripRequired. Band for sections; strip for bars and dividers.
data-geo-thememidnight (default) | blue | navy | ir | markets | noneSets the background and shape brightness. none keeps the element's own background.
data-geo-scalenumber, e.g. 1.3Optional. Scales all shapes.
data-geo-staticpresent / absentOptional. Draws the shapes without motion.

Still images

One frame of the same pattern, for places where motion is not wanted or not possible: page heroes, slides, social images, email headers. SVG scales to any size; WebP is for tools that need a raster image. Use with background-size:cover.
