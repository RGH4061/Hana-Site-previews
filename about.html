Why Hana Group

Hana brings semiconductor assembly and test together with full electronics manufacturing: OSAT and EMS in one independent, publicly listed company, across four countries. Specialized in microelectronics since 1978.

Contact us Explore capabilities
Vision

To be the electronics manufacturing services partner of choice, delivering high-quality microelectronics manufacturing across a broad field of related disciplines.

Mission

To build long-term value for shareholders, customers, and employees through strong customer satisfaction, the wellbeing of our people, responsible environmental and social practices, and a culture of innovation.

Vertically Integrated Operations

Many manufacturers can do one or the other but Hana does both. OSAT (outsourced semiconductor assembly and test) packages and tests the chips. EMS (electronics manufacturing services) builds the circuit boards and finished products around them. With both inside one company, a product that needs chip-level packaging and board-level assembly can move through both without leaving Hana.

For a buyer, that means fewer suppliers to qualify and manage, design and intellectual property kept within a single organization, and closer control over quality, cost, and schedule.

Manufacturing across four countries

Our global manufacturing footprint within Hana gives customers opportunities for program to be dual-sourced across borders without changing suppliers, closer to final markets and adaptable to different wage and technological requirements between countries.

Hana gives customers room to manage regional risk (tariffs, demand swings and supply disruption), including a domestic option in the United States and TAA compliant manufacturing in Cambodia.

Frequently asked questions

What is Hana Microelectronics?+
Hana Microelectronics is an electronics manufacturing services (EMS) and outsourced semiconductor assembly and test (OSAT) company headquartered in Bangkok, Thailand. It was founded in 1978 and manufactures for its customers at plants in Thailand, China, Cambodia and the USA.
Is Hana Microelectronics a publicly listed company?+
Yes. Hana Microelectronics Public Company Limited has been listed on the Stock Exchange of Thailand since 1993 under the ticker HANA, and it operates as an independent company.
Where does Hana manufacture?+
At five plants: Lamphun and Ayutthaya in Thailand, Jiaxing in China, Koh Kong in Cambodia and Solon, Ohio in the USA. A program can be dual-sourced from more than one country within the same company, with each plant qualified separately against the customer's requirements.
What is the difference between EMS and OSAT, and does Hana do both?+
OSAT covers packaging and testing semiconductor die as finished ICs. EMS covers assembling components onto circuit boards and building those boards into finished products. Hana does both within one company, so a device can be packaged, tested and then built into a board or product by the same manufacturer.
How many people work at Hana?+
Around 7,500 people across Hana's plants and offices.
