Draft · Capabilities hub motion

Square → Hana mark → capabilities

Three entrance variations for the hub on capabilities.html. Each starts from a solid Hana-blue square, opens into the mark, then places the six capability cards. Each plays once when it scrolls into view.

Variation A

Unfold

The square settles, splits into four quadrants that swing outward and dissolve, and the mark grows out of the gap. Cards are pushed out from the centre, then the connectors draw back to the mark.

Variation B

Trace

The mark's strokes draw in on their own, then the cards sweep clockwise along an orbit into place and the connectors draw back to the mark.

Variation C

Aperture

The square turns as it opens, rounding into the outer ring, and the mark is revealed through it like a lens. Cards sweep clockwise along an orbit into place.
