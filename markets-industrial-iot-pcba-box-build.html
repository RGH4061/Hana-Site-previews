PCBA and box build for industrial equipment

Hana manufactures industrial PCBAs for equipment operating in cabinets, production environments and outdoor enclosures, with processes available for heat, vibration, moisture and contamination requirements. Capabilities include conformal and parylene coating, mixed-technology SMT and through-hole assembly, selective and wave soldering, inspection and functional test.

What we specialize in

The same manufacturing capability also supports high-density PCBAs for infrastructure and data-center equipment, using fine-pitch SMT, inspection and X-ray processes for dense multi-layer assemblies.

01Ruggedized PCBAs
Surface-mount and mixed-technology PCBAs with conformal or parylene coating, through-hole assembly for high-current or mechanically loaded connections, and selective or wave soldering according to design requirements.
02Complete box-build units
PCBA integrated with enclosure, wiring, cable assemblies and mechanical components, followed by finished-product functional test.
03High density PCBA
Dense multi-layer PCBAs for infrastructure and data-center hardware, manufactured on fine-pitch SMT lines with X-ray inspection for hidden solder joints.

See PCBA & Box Build for the wider product-assembly process, and SMT Assembly for fine-pitch placement, inspection and test capability.

Coating, joints and inspection

Industrial PCBAs can require additional protection against moisture, vibration, thermal cycling and mechanical loading. Hana applies conformal or parylene coating where specified, with through-hole assembly used for high-current or mechanically loaded connections and the soldering process selected to suit the design. AOI and X-ray inspection provide coverage of visible and hidden joints, with in-circuit and functional testing available according to program requirements. For long-running products, controlled change management and obsolescence support help maintain manufacturing consistency throughout the product lifecycle.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What industrial products can Hana build?

Hana manufactures control boards and complete assemblies for industrial controllers, meters, monitors and connected equipment. Products can be supplied as PCBAs or as complete, tested units depending on program requirements.

Can Hana build the complete unit, not just the board?

Yes. Box build covers the enclosure, wiring and mechanical assembly around the board, followed by functional test of the finished product.

What protection can Hana apply for harsh environments?

Conformal and parylene coating are available, together with mixed-technology assembly and through-hole processes for joints carrying higher current or mechanical load. Inspection and functional test coverage are selected according to the program and confirmed per site.

Can Hana build high density PCBAs for infrastructure and data-center equipment?

Yes. Hana manufactures dense, multi-layer PCBAs for infrastructure and data-center hardware using fine-pitch SMT lines with X-ray inspection for hidden joints. For AI-infrastructure work specifically, see our Data Centers market.
