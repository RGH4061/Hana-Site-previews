RFID tire tags

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is a tire tag built to take being embedded in a tire?

Tire tags use rubber-compatible materials and encapsulation selected for integration into the tire and exposure to the curing process. Read performance can be verified after cure as part of the manufacturing flow.

How does tire-tag manufacturing relate to Hana's wider RFID capability?

Tire tags use the same core inlay, strap-attach and roll-to-roll manufacturing capability as Hana's wider RFID portfolio, with additional material and process requirements for embedding and cure.

Where does Hana build tire tags?

Tire-tag manufacturing capability is available across Hana's RFID operations in China and the USA, with the applicable site confirmed per program.
