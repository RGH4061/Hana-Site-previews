# IoT device assembly — from board to connected product

**Industrial & IoT · IoT Device Assembly**

A connected device is an integration problem before it is a board problem. The radio, the antenna, the sensors, the battery and the enclosure only meet each other once they are inside the same housing, and the faults that appear at that point are the expensive ones to find late. Hana assembles the whole unit rather than handing a board across: PCBA with embedded wireless modules, antenna and sensor integration, battery and harness, enclosure and final mechanical assembly — then functional test on the assembled product, not on the bare board. Wireless test is done in-house. Box build has run on our lines for years, alongside our industrial and medical work.

## What we specialize in
- **Complete connected devices** — board, radio, sensors, battery and enclosure built and tested as one finished, shippable unit.
- **Board, radio and antenna integration** — PCBA carrying embedded wireless modules, with antenna and sensor integration handled on the same line as the board build.
- **Enclosure, cable and harness assembly** — cable and harness build, enclosure fitting and final mechanical assembly around the board.
- **Wireless functional test** — 802.11 embedded wireless module test, GSM/cellular and GPS test, and Bluetooth and acoustic test, with the test set selected per program.

## Test the product, not the board

Wireless is where a device build most often comes apart. A radio that passes at board level can behave differently once its antenna sits alongside a battery, a metal bracket and a molded housing — and by then the assembly is closed. Hana's answer is to test the assembled unit. In-house wireless functional test covers 802.11 embedded modules, GSM and cellular, GPS, and Bluetooth and acoustic rigs, selected to what the program actually needs to prove. Builds can be run at pilot and pre-production quantities before volume, so the assembly sequence and its test coverage are settled on real units rather than on paper.

## Where we build
Connected-device assembly runs across Hana sites in **Thailand, China and Cambodia** — PCBA and box build at Lamphun and Jiaxing, final assembly, cable and harness work at Koh Kong. Because the same capability runs in more than one country inside a single company, a program can be dual-sourced for capacity or supply-chain resilience — geographic optionality without leaving Hana.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can Hana assemble my whole IoT device, not just the PCB?**
Yes. Box build covers the radio, sensors, battery, cable and harness, and the enclosure and mechanical parts around the board, assembled and functionally tested as one finished unit before it ships.

**How can Hana test a wireless product before it ships?**
On the assembled device rather than the board. Wireless functional test available in-house covers 802.11 embedded modules, GSM and cellular, GPS, and Bluetooth and acoustic test. The set applied is selected per program and confirmed per site.

**Can Hana support a pilot build before volume production?**
Yes. Pilot and pre-production builds can be run ahead of volume so the assembly sequence, the fixtures and the test coverage are proven on real units first.
