# PCBA and box build for industrial equipment

**Industrial & IoT · PCBA & Box Build**

Industrial boards run in cabinets, on plant floors and inside outdoor enclosures, where heat, vibration, moisture and airborne contamination are constant rather than occasional conditions. Hana assembles to that environment: conformal and parylene coating, mixed-technology assembly that combines surface-mount with through-hole where a joint has to carry current or take mechanical load, selective and wave soldering, and inspection on every board. Industrial also covers a wider span than plant equipment alone — the same lines build the dense, high density PCBAs that go into infrastructure and data-centre hardware. Both have run on our floors for years, alongside our automotive and medical work.

## What we build

- **Ruggedised PCBAs** — surface-mount and mixed-technology boards with conformal or parylene coating, through-hole for high-current and heavy connectors, and selective or wave soldering to suit the joint.
- **Complete box-build units** — the board integrated with its enclosure, wiring and mechanical parts, assembled and functionally tested as one finished product.
- **High density PCBA** — dense, multi-layer boards for infrastructure and data-centre equipment, built on the same fine-pitch SMT lines with X-ray on the joints a dense layout hides.

## Coating, joints and inspection

The failure modes in a high-stress environment are well understood — moisture tracking across a board, a solder joint fatigued by vibration, a heavy connector working loose under load. Hana answers each of them with process rather than assumption. Coating is applied and verified for coverage. Through-hole is specified where a joint carries current or takes mechanical strain, with the soldering method matched to it. Automated optical inspection and X-ray check the joints that cannot be seen by eye, and in-circuit and functional test confirm the assembly before it leaves the line. Where a programme runs over several years, change control and obsolescence management keep the build consistent as components move through their own life cycles.

## One company, more than one country

**What industrial products can Hana build?**
Control boards and the equipment built around them — industrial controllers, meters, monitors and connected equipment — supplied as bare PCBAs or as complete, tested units.
